## SOP Sample Set — Summit Comfort Heating & Air {#sop-sample-set-summit-comfort-heating-air}

Fictional company built as a portfolio sample for the SOP & Operations Documentation service ($97–$175). Three documents: customer call script, technician daily report template, and complaint handling procedure.

* * *

## Document 1: Customer Call Script — Incoming Service Requests {#doc-1-customer-call-script}

**Purpose:** Standardize how dispatch handles incoming calls so every customer gets a consistent, professional experience and dispatch captures everything a technician needs before arriving on\-site.

**Owner:** Dispatch / Customer Service
**Applies to:** All incoming calls for service requests, quotes, and emergencies

### Step 1 — Greeting {#step-1-greeting}

"Thank you for calling Summit Comfort Heating and Air, this is \[Name\], how can I help you today?"

### Step 2 — Identify call type {#step-2-identify-call-type}

Ask: "Is this for a repair, a maintenance visit, or a new installation quote?"

Route based on answer:

| Call type | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Repair (system not working) | Go to Step 3 — Emergency Screening |
| Maintenance / tune\-up | Go to Step 4 — Standard Scheduling |
| New install / quote | Transfer to Sales, or take contact info if Sales unavailable |

### Step 3 — Emergency screening (repairs only) {#step-3-emergency-screening-repairs-only}

Ask these three questions, in order:

1. "Is there any smell of gas, or smoke?" — If yes, instruct the customer to leave the house immediately and call the gas company/911. Do not schedule a visit. Log as EMERGENCY — GAS.
2. "Is anyone in the home elderly, an infant, or medically vulnerable to extreme temperature?" — If yes, flag as PRIORITY.
3. "What is the current indoor temperature, if you know it?" — Record for the technician.

### Step 4 — Standard scheduling {#step-4-standard-scheduling}

Collect, in order:

1. Full name and service address
2. Best callback number
3. Description of the issue in the customer's own words (write it verbatim — do not paraphrase)
4. Preferred appointment window (offer two options, do not ask "when works for you" open\-ended)
5. Confirm: "You're all set for \[day/window\]. A technician will call 20–30 minutes before arrival. Is there anything else I can help with?"

### Step 5 — Close {#step-5-close}

"Thank you for choosing Summit Comfort — you'll get a text confirmation shortly."

**Never say:** "I don't know" without following up with "let me find out and call you back within the hour." Never give a repair cost estimate over the phone — that's the technician's call after diagnosis.
