# Customer Onboarding SOP
MainByte Labs | Version 1.0 | Last Updated: August 2026

---

## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for onboarding new customers at a 
service-based business. It ensures every new client receives a consistent, 
professional experience from first contact to active service delivery.

---

## Scope

Applies to all staff responsible for client intake, account setup, and 
initial service delivery.

---

## Prerequisites

- CRM access (e.g. HubSpot, Zoho, or spreadsheet-based tracker)
- Client contract template
- Welcome email template
- Internal project folder structure

---

## Onboarding Process

### Step 1 — Confirm and Document the Sale

1. Mark the lead as **Closed/Won** in your CRM
2. Create a new client record with the following fields:
   - Full name and business name
   - Primary contact email and phone
   - Service purchased and start date
   - Assigned team member or point of contact
3. Create a client folder in your file system:
/Clients/[ClientName]/
/Contracts
/Communications
/Deliverables

---

### Step 2 — Send the Welcome Email

Send within **24 hours** of confirmed sale.

**Email must include:**
- Confirmation of service purchased
- Expected start date
- What the client needs to provide (assets, access, information)
- Your direct contact information
- Next scheduled touchpoint (call, meeting, or check-in)

---

### Step 3 — Collect Client Information

Schedule a 30-minute intake call or send an intake form covering:

- Business overview and goals
- Current pain points the service should address
- Tools and software currently in use
- Preferred communication method and frequency
- Any deadlines or hard constraints

Document all responses in the client record.

---

### Step 4 — Set Up Internal Project Tracking

1. Create a project entry in your task management tool
2. Add the following default tasks:
   - [ ] Intake call completed
   - [ ] Assets/access received
   - [ ] Kickoff deliverable due date set
   - [ ] First check-in scheduled
3. Set the project status to **Active**

---

### Step 5 — Deliver Kickoff Confirmation

Send a short follow-up message (email or message) confirming:
- All information has been received
- Work is starting on [date]
- Next update will be on [date]

---

## Escalation

If a client does not respond to intake requests within **3 business days**:
1. Send one follow-up message
2. If no response after 5 business days — flag to owner/manager for review
