# Notion Workspace Setup Guide
**MainByte Labs — Sample Document**
Version 1.0 | Last Updated: August 2026

---

## Purpose

This guide walks a small team through setting up a Notion workspace for 
day-to-day project tracking, documentation, and communication. No prior 
Notion experience required.

---

## Prerequisites

- A Notion account (free plan works — sign up at notion.so)
- Admin access to your workspace
- 30–45 minutes to complete initial setup

---

## Section 1 — Create and Configure Your Workspace

### 1.1 Create a New Workspace

1. Log in to Notion at [notion.so](https://notion.so)
2. Click your workspace name in the top-left corner
3. Select **Join or create workspace**
4. Choose **Create a new workspace**
5. Enter your business name and click **Create**

### 1.2 Set Workspace Preferences

1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar
2. Under **Workspace Settings**, configure:
   - Workspace name (use your business name)
   - Workspace icon (upload a logo or choose an emoji)
   - Default timezone

---

## Section 2 — Build Your Core Structure

Set up the following four pages as your base structure. Each becomes a 
section your team navigates daily.

### 2.1 Home Page

Create a page called **🏠 Home**. Use it as a dashboard — add links to 
your most-used pages, upcoming tasks, and any pinned announcements.

**To create a page:**
1. Click **+** next to your workspace name in the sidebar
2. Title the page and choose an icon
3. Use `/` to insert blocks (text, tables, links, etc.)

### 2.2 Projects Page

Create a page called **📋 Projects**. Inside, create a **Database (Table)** 
with the following columns:

| Column | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Project Name | Title | Name of the project |
| Status | Select | Not Started / In Progress / Done |
| Owner | Person | Team member responsible |
| Due Date | Date | Target completion date |
| Notes | Text | Any additional context |

### 2.3 SOPs and Docs Page

Create a page called **📁 SOPs & Docs**. Use this as your internal 
knowledge base. Add sub-pages for each process or reference document 
your team uses regularly.

### 2.4 Team Inbox Page

Create a page called **📬 Team Inbox**. Use this for async updates, 
questions, and notes that don't belong in a specific project.

---

## Section 3 — Invite Your Team

1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar
2. Select **Members**
3. Click **Add members**
4. Enter each team member's email address
5. Set their role:
   - **Member** — can view and edit
   - **Guest** — limited access to specific pages only
   - **Admin** — full workspace control (limit to 1–2 people)

---

## Section 4 — Daily Use Tips

- Use `/` on any page to insert tables, checklists, dividers, and more
- Use **@** to mention a team member and notify them directly
- Use **[[** to link to another page inside your workspace
- Pin your most-used pages to the sidebar by right-clicking and selecting 
  **Add to sidebar**

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| Team member can't see a page | Check their role and page permissions under **Share** |
| Database not showing all entries | Check if a filter is active — click **Filter** to review |
| Page deleted by accident | Go to **Settings > Teamspace** and check the trash |
