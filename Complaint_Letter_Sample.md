Dear Harwick Customer Service Team,

I am writing to formally request a full refund of $347.99 for a Harwick ProDry 
6000 countertop dryer purchased on February 14, 2024 through your website. The 
unit stopped heating entirely after eleven days of normal household use.

I contacted your support line on February 26 (reference #SR-44021) and was 
advised to allow the unit to "reset" over 48 hours. I followed that instruction. 
The problem did not resolve. A second call on March 1 resulted in a replacement 
heating element being shipped, which arrived damaged. I have photographs of the 
damaged part and the original unit's error display, available on request.

At this point I have spent three weeks, two support calls, and one failed parts 
shipment attempting to resolve what should have been a straightforward warranty 
claim. I am not writing out of frustration alone — I am writing because I want 
a clear path to resolution and I believe you can provide one.

Under the implied warranty of merchantability recognized in most U.S. states, a 
product that fails within two weeks of normal use has not met the basic standard 
of fitness for its intended purpose. I am not asking for anything beyond what 
your own 30-day return policy already covers.

I am requesting:

1. A full refund of $347.99 to the original payment method within 10 business days
2. A prepaid return shipping label for the defective unit

If I do not receive a response within 10 business days of this letter, I will 
file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and my state 
Attorney General's consumer protection office, and dispute the charge with my 
credit card issuer under the Fair Credit Billing Act.

I would genuinely prefer to resolve this with you directly. I have been a 
Harwick customer for several years and this is the first issue I have had. I 
hope we can close this cleanly.
