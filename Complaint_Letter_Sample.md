**RE: Formal Complaint and Demand for Refund — Order \#HH\-88234\-921**

To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing to formally dispute Hearth & Home Furnishings' failure to deliver merchandise I purchased and paid for in full, and to demand a complete refund within fourteen (14) days of the date of this letter.

**Summary of Facts**

On June 12, 2026, I placed an order for one Camden Sectional Sofa (Order \#HH\-88234\-921) through hearthandhomefurnishings.com, and paid $1,240.00 in full at checkout using my Visa card ending in 4471. The order confirmation email stated an estimated delivery window of 4–6 weeks, placing delivery no later than July 24, 2026.

That date passed without delivery or communication from your company. I contacted customer support on August 1, August 15, and August 29, 2026. On each occasion I was told the order was "delayed in transit" and given no revised delivery date. As of the date of this letter, more than twelve weeks after purchase, the item has not been delivered, and no refund has been issued.

**Applicable Law**

Under the Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 435), a seller who cannot ship merchandise within the promised time — or within 30 days if no time was promised — must either obtain the buyer's consent to the delay or provide a full refund. Hearth & Home Furnishings did neither: I was not asked to consent to any delay, and no refund has been offered.

This conduct also constitutes an unfair or deceptive act or practice under Ohio's Consumer Sales Practices Act, Ohio Revised Code § 1345.01 et seq., which prohibits suppliers from accepting payment for goods they do not deliver as represented.

**Demand**

I am requesting the following:

1. A full refund of $1,240.00, issued to the Visa card used for the original purchase.
2. Written confirmation of the refund, including the date it was processed, sent to the email address above.

Please complete this refund within fourteen (14) days of the date of this letter. If I do not receive confirmation of a full refund by **September 21, 2026**, I will file complaints with the Ohio Attorney General's Consumer Protection Section and the Federal Trade Commission, and will pursue all remedies available to me, including a chargeback through my card issuer and a claim in small claims court.

I would prefer to resolve this matter directly and promptly. Please contact me at the phone number or email above to confirm the refund has been processed.

Sincerely,

Jordan Ellis
